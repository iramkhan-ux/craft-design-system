# Guardrails: Elevation

All rules below were confirmed by Iram Khan (iramkhan-ux) on 2026-10-05. Source: Elevation foundation, decision 0005. Scope: system-wide.

| # | Rule | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Designers use the elevation styles only. No hand-made shadows. | Keeps every raised surface consistent and lets a change reach everything. |
| 2 | Shadow colors come from the tokens. Nobody picks a shadow color by hand. | The shadow color changes with light and dark mode. A hand-picked color would not. |
