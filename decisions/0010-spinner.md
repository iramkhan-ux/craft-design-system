## Context

First component of the Craft pilot, and the first built on the foundations. It is an atomic component, so it comes before Button and IconButton. Values come from Blade source code, which is the final source of truth (see [0004](0004-color-from-blade-source.md)).

Source: `razorpay/blade`, commit `e914cf6a843193dc5f259aae27027c1dbb3544ea`, folder `packages/blade/src/components/Spinner` (`Spinner`, `BaseSpinner`, `SpinnerIcon`, `SpinningBox`, `spinnerTokens`).

## Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Spinner has no tokens of its own. It reuses the icon sizes and the interactive icon colors. Decided by the designer. |
| 2 | Sizes keep Blade's names: `medium` (16px), `large` (20px), `xlarge` (24px). They are the `medium`, `large` and `xlarge` icon sizes. Decided by the designer. |
| 3 | Four colors, as in Blade: `neutral` (default, `interactive.icon.gray.muted`), `primary` (`interactive.icon.primary.subtle`), `white` (`interactive.icon.staticWhite.subtle`) and `onNeutral` (`interactive.icon.onNeutral.normal`). |
| 4 | Optional label, `right` (default) or `bottom`, in the Body / Small / Regular text style, color `surface.text.gray.muted`, with `spacing.3` between the spinner and the label. |
| 5 | Screen readers get a `progressbar` role. The announced text is `aria-label`, else the label, else "Loading". |
| 6 | Motion: one full turn every `duration.2xgentle` (960ms) with `easing.overshoot`, forever, as in Blade. Static in Figma; it turns only in code and Storybook. Decided by the designer. |
| 7 | With reduced motion on, the Spinner keeps turning at half the pace (1920ms). Blade does not do this. Decided by the designer. |
| 8 | Figma: one component set `Spinner` with the variants `Size`, `Color` and `Label position` (24 variants, checksum `e45126b9`) plus a `Show label` switch and a `Label` text. Size and color are bound to the icon-size and icon color variables. |

## Exceptions and oddities

- Blade's spinner drawing is a faint ring (20% opacity) with two solid arcs. It is kept as is, on a 24 by 24 grid, with 1px of padding around it (as in Blade), so the full size is 18, 22 and 26px.
- The reduced-motion pace is the duration token times two. That is a calculation on a token, not a new value.
- The `white` color is the same in light and dark, so use it only on dark or colored surfaces. `onNeutral` follows the theme and is for filled neutral surfaces.
- Blade's `BaseSpinner` also accepts feedback colors. Only the four in the public `Spinner` are built.
